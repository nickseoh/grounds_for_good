What could your church space become?

Most pastors start inside the building: a daycare, nonprofit offices, tutoring, a senior program. Some look at the parking lot or a field for housing. This copilot helps you compare both, then shows the rules, the partners, and the pitch.

Findings cite only the zoning text you supply. Anything else is marked “Unverified” for your planner or attorney to confirm.

      Churches that have done this

      • Solid Rock Christian Center (Colorado Springs) formed its own community development corporation and opened a 77-unit affordable building on a field next to the church in 2024. Colorado Politics
      • Woodmen Valley Chapel (Colorado Springs) hosts Sunrise at Shiloh Mesa, 50 senior apartments built by Greccio Housing with tax credits. Colorado Politics

      • Metro Denver churches lease land to Habitat for Humanity for for-sale homes. Denver7
